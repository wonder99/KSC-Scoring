A quick guide for players

🎯 KSC Darts Scorer

Keep one shared score for your team, rotate through four players, and finish each game on a double.

Open the scorer

One team, one scorekeeper: this app tracks your team’s score only. Use one phone as the scorekeeper for your team; the opposing team can track its own score separately.

1. Start a match

  1. Open the scorer and choose the match day. It defaults to today if scheduled, or the most recent past scheduled day.
  2. Choose your team. The app looks up the opponent and board automatically.
  3. Tap Start Game. The first four roster players start above the divider in Playing this game, and exactly four names always stay there. Drag an unselected name into its desired position; the fourth selected player moves below the divider. Their top-to-bottom order is the play order. To move a selected player down, drop them on the desired row; the names in between shift up. On a phone, use Add to replace the fourth player, Replace to swap a selected player with the first unselected player, and the arrows to set play order.
  4. An optional Spare player is available. If selected, Spare’s turn starts with 25 points entered. Tap Submit Turn to add those points.
  5. Tap Start Game again to begin. Each game starts at 701. There are seven games per selected fixture; choose a lineup again after each game.

2. Enter each turn

Enter a turn total Type the player’s total into Turn Total, then tap Submit Turn. The field is focused after each submission so you can type the next score right away.
Or score dart by dart Open Dart Grid. Choose Single, Double, or Triple, then tap a number from 1–20. You can enter up to three darts per turn.

Bull and Miss

  • Bull: Single scores 25; Double scores 50. Bull is unavailable in Triple mode.
  • Miss: records zero for that dart. A turn of three misses can still be submitted.

The score history lists each player on their own row. Scores of 95 or more are highlighted.

3. Finish the game

  • You must reach exactly zero with the last dart in a double or the inner bull (50). A checkout must be entered using the dart grid so the app can verify the finishing dart.
  • A score that goes below zero, leaves one, or reaches zero without a double-out is a bust. A bust does not reduce your team’s score.
  • Checkout suggestions appear when 170 or fewer points remain. The suggestions follow a doubling-out chart.
  • If the opponent doubles out first, tap [opponent] has doubled out! and confirm. The game will be recorded as an opponent win.
  • Bust / Skip ends the current player’s turn without subtracting points.

4. Review and share results

After a game ends, the result screen offers:

  • Save to League stores the match results on the league server. Enter the league upload key and save after each game; recorded turn scores are included even when your team loses.
  • Choose lineup returns to player selection for the next game.

To download saved results, select Browse uploaded scores on the scorer page (or open /uploads on the Node Web Service). Enter the league upload key, select one or more match/team uploads, and download the combined CSV.

5. Use the scorer on a phone

  1. Open the link shared by the organizer in Safari or Chrome.
  2. For quicker access, use the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen. This creates a shortcut to the website, not an offline app.
  3. Keep the page open during the match. Do not refresh or close it before downloading the results.

An internet connection is required to open the app and load the roster and schedule. Scores are not synchronized between phones or saved to an account. Refreshing or closing the page loses the current session.

For the organizer: putting it online

This is a static website; it does not need a server-side program or build step. A simple option is Netlify Drop: sign in, then drag a folder containing index.html, app.js, styles.css, team_lists.csv, dart_league_schedule.csv, and this guide onto the upload page. Share the resulting website link with players. Upload the updated files again whenever the roster or schedule changes.

The roster and schedule CSV files are served to anyone who can access the site. Do not publish names or other information unless the group is comfortable with it being publicly accessible. A link alone does not provide password protection.
